# Phase B — on-device checklist (Pixel 9, Chrome)

Spec §13, Phase B: Learn for Stance, Guard, Jab and Cross.

Before you start:
- Tap **Refresh** on the update toast.
- Turn on **Settings → Debug log → Record**, so anything odd can be replayed.
- Set up at **30°** with the phone at waist height, about 3 m in front of you and 1.5 m to your rear-hand side. Face a spot on the wall, not the phone.

## 0. Re-calibrate at 30°

Run **Home → Setup → Re-calibrate**. Your saved guard and reach were measured facing the phone, and the new checks compare against them. The jab step should count your jabs within a few seconds each.

## 1. Stance and Guard (Learn)

Each is 3 sets of a 20-second hold. After each set the app speaks a summary, then starts the next set on its own after 25 seconds.
- Hold properly through all 3 sets. Expect mostly green in the strip, and both techniques should become **Solid**. That unlocks Jab and Cross.
- In one set, drop your hands for 2 seconds or more. Expect **"Hands up"** within 2 seconds, and that window turns amber.

## 2. Jab (Learn)

Throw one jab on each tick, 8 per set.

| Try this | Expect |
|---|---|
| 8 normal jabs | 8 tones: a chime for clean, a soft note for good, a low thud for fix. No missed reps. **Exit: ≥ 90% detected.** |
| Drop your rear hand on 5 of the 8 jabs | The summary ends "Next set: rear glove glued to your cheek." **Exit criterion.** |
| Let your hands drop between jabs for 2 seconds | "Hands up" within 2 seconds. **Exit criterion.** |
| Throw crosses instead | "I asked for a jab — that looked like a cross." |

## 3. Cross (Learn)

| Try this | Expect |
|---|---|
| 8 crosses with a good back-foot pivot | Mostly chimes. |
| 8 crosses with your back foot flat | Soft notes (good, not clean), and "Squash the bug under your back foot." |

**Exit criterion:** jab and cross told apart at least 90% of the time. Watch for "that looked like a jab" during the cross sets.

## 4. Idle

Stand in your stance for 60 seconds during a Jab set, without punching after a tick. Expect no clean, good or fix tones, only "missed" reps (silent). **Exit: 0 false reps.**

## What to send

- The debug log, from **Settings → Download log**, attached here. It keeps only the last 4 minutes, so download it after each technique.
- Anything that felt wrong:
  - a tone that didn't match what you did
  - a summary that didn't fit
  - the timing of the ticks
